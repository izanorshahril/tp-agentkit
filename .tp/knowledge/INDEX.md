# Living Knowledge

Knowledge records verified facts and decision-changing lessons, not procedures or task history. Procedures belong in skills; current preferences, environment facts, checkpoints, and raw outputs belong in `.tp/work/`.

## Topics

| Topic | Load when |
|---|---|
| [`test-programs.md`](test-programs.md) | mapping or modifying an unfamiliar ATE test program, especially T2000-style revisions, limits, flows, and runtime coverage |

## Promotion rule

Create or update a focused topic only when completed work supplies evidence for a reusable fact that would change a future agent's decision. Record scope, confidence, and the evidence shape without private identifiers. One example can justify a narrow lesson; broader rules require repeated or authoritative support.

Keep the index short. Remove or merge entries whose trigger overlaps and whose contents can share one coherent topic.
